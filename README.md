### Customer Purchase Prediction System

I developed a machine learning system that predicts whether a customer is likely to make a purchase or remain a viewer based on their behavioral and demographic data.

The project involved analyzing customer data to identify patterns and factors associated with purchasing behavior. I cleaned and prepared the dataset, performed exploratory data analysis, analyzed relationships between customer characteristics and purchase decisions, and prepared the relevant features for machine learning.

I then trained and evaluated classification models to predict the likelihood of a customer making a purchase. The goal was to transform customer data into actionable insights that could help businesses identify potential buyers, better understand customer behavior, and improve marketing and sales strategies.

This project demonstrates my skills in Python, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, feature engineering, classification, machine learning, and business-focused data analysis.

**Tools & Technologies:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, and Jupyter Notebook.

**Key Skills:** Customer Analytics, Predictive Analytics, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, Feature Engineering, Classification, Machine Learning, and Business Intelligence.
